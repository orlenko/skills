# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One technical operator supervising roughly five to ten Claude and Codex sessions
across local software projects. They work primarily in terminals, switch context
frequently, and need a glanceable way to notice decisions, completed turns,
stalled work, observer failures, and potentially abandoned conversational threads.

## Product Purpose

Agent Observer provides trustworthy visibility into agent work without requiring
worker cooperation. It gathers read-only evidence from local session traces,
separates observed facts from model suggestions, and gives the operator one place
to decide which original session deserves attention. Success means the operator
can recover situational awareness quickly and trust both each claim and its stated
limits.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and quietly vigilant. The product should feel like a dependable
instrument beside the user's terminals, not another conversational personality
competing for attention.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic infrastructure-monitoring wall, a chat transcript
inbox, or a celebratory project-management board. Avoid decorative agent avatars,
gamified activity, noisy equal-weight alert cards, invented certainty, and any
layout that makes model inference look as authoritative as source-backed facts.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with attention, not activity volume.
2. Make provenance and uncertainty inspectable at the point of every claim.
3. Preserve independent facets instead of collapsing a project into one status.
4. Use progressive disclosure so five to ten projects remain calm at a glance.
5. Return the operator to the original worker session; never impersonate it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must be keyboard accessible, preserve
visible focus, support reduced motion, remain understandable without color, and
work at narrow laptop widths and high browser zoom. Exact evidence must remain
selectable and readable with assistive technology.
